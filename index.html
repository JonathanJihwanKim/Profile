Traces DAX measure dependencies from visuals to source columns in PBIP projects. Includes commit-by-commit change intelligence across 30+ change types. Essential for teams adopting Git-based workflows.

Problem

DAX refactoring in large PBIP projects was blind, no way to know which visuals would break when renaming or removing a column or measure.

Approach

Built a dependency tracer that maps DAX lineage from visuals to source columns, plus commit-by-commit git intelligence across 30+ change types.

Impact
